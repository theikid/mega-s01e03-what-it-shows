Counted as the desk's own view counts: a one-day move stays in the count of the cause it is filed under. The export has 427 rows for 412 reports: its pages overlap, and a report is counted once, by id. The 412 reports carry 12 different subject lines.

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. A tenant that writes the day first gets the day and the month swapped. Reports from the app, from email, and from the booking-v2 form. This is what #4471 says it is.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type the month first, as told, and the form reads the day first. The result looks the same, a swapped date, but the bug is in the form’s hint, not in the parser. Fixing the parser does not fix it.
  • one-day-move: the date moves by one day, not by a month. One tenant sees it only in one month; one sees two clients in two time zones get two different days for the same meeting. Nothing is swapped. The desk’s own notes list these reports, and they stayed filed under parser because of the tenant they came from.

How each report was counted

  1. Take every report merged into #4471, once per id.
  2. Count it under its cause, as causes.ts gives it: hint when it came through the form of a day-first tenant on booking-v3, parser otherwise. This is the desk’s own view.
  3. The reports the desk notes list as a one-day move get a ticket of their own. They stay in the count of the cause they are filed under, as in the desk’s view, and are counted again on their own row.

The intake tag is not used. It is a guess from the reporter’s browser, and the table above counts how often it differs from the cause.

Why it was merged

Decision 0001 merges on the subject line alone. The same subject line appears on reports with different problems, so a rule that never reads the body cannot keep them apart. The page was written from the merge, and nobody looked at what it showed.

Where each problem stands

Engineering closed #4471 on 5 October. Its update names two of the three problems:

  • parser: fixed, with fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. Ticket closed as done.
  • hint: won’t fix, because the booking-v3 form is retired. Ticket closed as not planned.
  • one-day-move: not in the update. A fix that parses the day first does not move a date by one day, so this ticket stays open.
